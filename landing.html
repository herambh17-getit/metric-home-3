Why Malvan · Tarkarli

The cleanest stretch of the Konkan coast — and the fastest-improving corner of it.

Tarkarli's white sand and clear water sit minutes away; Sindhudurg Fort is a short boat ride from Malvan jetty. With Chipi airport operational and NH-66 upgraded, Tarkarli has shifted from a season destination to a year-round rental and second-home market.

Straight answers

The questions buyers are afraid to ask.

Is Horizon Grande MahaRERA registered, and can I verify it myself?
Yes. Horizon Grande is registered under MahaRERA P52900048510, and registration is completed before any marketing begins. Verify the number yourself on the MahaRERA portal, or ask us for the certificate — we'll send it on WhatsApp without a form. Payments go into a RERA-designated account and are released against verified milestones.
What is the payment plan, and are there hidden charges?
The price we quote is all-in — base cost, stamp duty, registration, GST, advance maintenance and legal charges are on the table from day one, in a written cost sheet. Payments follow RERA-compliant milestones tied to verified construction stages. There are no floor-rise charges and no late "infrastructure" additions.
Can you help with a home loan?
Yes. We're empanelled with leading banks and housing finance companies, and your named manager coordinates eligibility, documentation and disbursement. We'll walk you through every clause in plain language — no jargon, no rush.
When is possession?
Towers 1 and 2 are ready to move in today. Tower 3 is on schedule for handover in December 2026. We publish dated construction photos every week against the MahaRERA milestone plan. If anything ever slips, you hear it from us first — with the reason and a revised date.
Can I visit on a weekend? I'm coming from out of town.
Yes — site visits run seven days a week, and most of our buyers come on a weekend. Tell us your date on the form and we'll confirm on WhatsApp, plus help with travel and stay guidance for the drive down. If you can't travel yet, we'll do a live video walkthrough from the site instead.
Will the finished flat match what I'm shown?
Yes — and we prove it before you book. You walk the live construction, not a styled show flat. Every material is specified by brand, grade and series in your agreement, so nothing can be substituted on site. When we say 450 sq.ft. carpet, that is what MahaRERA records and what you walk into.

Still deciding? Talk to our site team today.

No pressure and no pitch — just the people who are actually building it. Ask about the slab schedule, the cost sheet, the paperwork, or whether a December handover is realistic. We'll answer straight.

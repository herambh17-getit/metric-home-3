Q1

What actually goes wrong when you buy a second home on the Konkan coast?

Three things, in this order: the price grows after you book, the handover date quietly moves, and the paperwork turns out to be someone else's problem.

You have probably heard the same sentences we have. "The rate is ₹X — we'll discuss the other charges later." Then the cost sheet arrives with floor rise, infrastructure charges, club fees and a maintenance advance nobody mentioned. "Possession by Diwali" becomes next Diwali, and then a phone that stops being answered. And the title, the NA order, the CRZ position, the RERA registration — all of it stays in a folder you are told about but never shown.

None of this is exotic. It is the ordinary experience of buying in a coastal market where most projects are small, most builders are local, and very few of them are registered. The cost of getting it wrong is not just money — it is years.

  • ₹2–6L The quiet overrun

    Typical spread between a headline "rate" and the real all-in cost once statutory and society charges land — money you had not budgeted.

  • 2–4 yrs The lost seasons

    A delayed handover is monsoons you did not spend there, and rental income the flat never earned while you paid EMIs.

  • Zero Recourse without RERA

    An unregistered project gives you no escrow, no published milestone plan and no regulator to complain to.
